The short version

Most people who build websites have never worked a Saturday in one.

I spent more than twenty years in hospitality and owned three cafés. I know what a quiet Wednesday feels like in the bank account on Thursday.

I also know what it's like to be sold marketing by someone who has never had to make payroll out of it. You hand over money you worked hard for and get a website that looks fine and does nothing.

The Service Edit started with email. My first project was setting up and launching Meat Merchant's email marketing in Klaviyo. Websites came next.

It's still just me. No account manager, no junior doing the build, no agency layer. You deal with the person doing the work, from the first conversation to the day the site goes live, and every email after that.

What the floor taught me

Three things I build around.

01

People read your site standing up.

Nobody browses a café website at a desk. They're on a phone, deciding where to go in the next twenty minutes. So I build for the phone first, and for the three things a customer is actually trying to do. Everything else gets out of the way.

02

Your regulars are already on a list.

Every booking, every order and every QR code on a table is a name you already have. Most businesses never send those people anything. That's why email sits next to every site I build.

03

Cheaper is sometimes right.

If you only need to exist online, I'll say so. You'll hear what your business actually needs, even when it's less than you expected to spend.

Straight answers

Before you get in touch.

Who runs The Service Edit?

Me, Mel Cox. The Service Edit is a one person studio based in Perth, Western Australia. You deal with me from the first email to launch day, and every email after that. There's no account manager and no junior doing the build.

What do you actually do?

Websites and email marketing for service businesses. Photo upgrades are part of every build, so your site launches with photos that match it. On the email side I set up your platform, write and send campaigns in your voice, and build the automated emails that run on their own: welcome, birthday and win back.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Hospitality, trades, e-commerce and venue businesses, and plenty that don't fit a category. So far that's a laneway café in Northbridge, a pizza and margarita bar in Casuarina, a global venue finding business, a hand-binding book studio in Wollongong and a handmade fishing lure maker.

Do you only work with Perth businesses?

No. I'm based in Perth and work with businesses right across Australia. CVBS finds venues worldwide.

Why does a café owner build websites?

Because I've been on the other side of the counter. Twenty years in hospitality and three cafés means I build for what a customer actually does on a phone, not for what looks good in a portfolio.

How do I start?

Send me your website and a line about your business. I'll go through it and tell you plainly what's costing you enquiries. If a new site isn't the answer, I'll say so.
