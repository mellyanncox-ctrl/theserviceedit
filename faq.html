FAQ

Questions

The things people actually ask, answered the way I would answer them on the phone.

How long does a site take?

About a week for a one page build once I have your photos. Gold Foil Books was five days from the first conversation to live. Bigger builds run in phases and I will tell you the timeline before we start.

What does it cost?

Websites from $499, one off. Email marketing from $350 a week. Both numbers are on the pricing page and they do not change depending on who is asking.

Do I have to write the copy?

No. That is the part most people dread and the reason most sites never get finished. I write it and you correct me.

What if I do not have good photos?

We work with what you have and I will tell you honestly which shots are letting the page down. A phone photo taken in the right light beats a stock image of somebody else’s business every time.

Who owns the site?

You do, outright, on your own domain. There is no platform subscription and no licence. If you never speak to me again the site keeps working.

Do you do social media or ads?

No. Websites and email only. Doing four things averagely is how most studios work and I would rather do two things properly.

Are you only in Perth?

I am based in Perth and work with businesses right across Australia. Current clients are in New South Wales, Western Australia and nationally.

What happens after the site goes live?

Nothing you have to pay for. If you want the emails running, that starts the same week. If you just want the site, it is done and it is yours.
