Questions · Straight Answers

The things people actually ask.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace?+
You can — and for some businesses that’s the right call. But template builders are designed to be easy for the owner, not convincing for the customer. You end up with the same layout as three of your competitors, slower load times, and hours of your own evenings you’ll never bill for. A custom site is built around how your customers decide. One decent job usually covers the difference.
How long does it take?+
A one-page site is usually live about five days after you approve the concept. Multi-page builds run two to four weeks. The honest bottleneck is content — the faster you send photos and details, the faster you’re live.
Can you redesign my current website?+
That’s most of the work we do. You’re not starting from zero — we keep what’s working, cut what’s costing you, and rebuild around it.
Do you write the copy?+
Yes, all of it. Most owners would rather not write about themselves — that’s normal. You get plain-English, trust-focused copy built around what customers need to hear before they call.
Will it work on mobile?+
It’s designed for mobile first, because that’s where most of your customers will meet you — fast, thumb-friendly, with the call button never more than a tap away.
Can I edit it later?+
It’s yours outright — domain, content, code. Small updates are covered by the care plan, or we hand everything over and you’re free to take it anywhere. No lock-in, ever.
How does hosting work?+
Two options. On the care plan, hosting, security, backups and updates are handled and you never think about it. Or we set everything up on accounts you own and hand over the keys.
What’s the free homepage concept, exactly?+
Before you spend a dollar, we design a custom homepage direction around your actual business — live and clickable, not a mood board. Love it, we build it. Don’t, and you’ve lost nothing but gained a clearer picture of where your business could go.
