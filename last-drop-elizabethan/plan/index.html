01Your first month, 25 November to Christmas Month one on results

Four Christmas campaigns plus your welcome email. Christmas functions go first, because groups of 20 or more prepay 7 days ahead and the best Fridays are going now.

How I've planned it

  • Your combined list is about 16,500 guests from Squarespace, me&u and NowBookit. Most of them have eaten or ordered with you before, so they already know the Lizzie.
  • Christmas functions are in three of the four emails, each with the real book-by dates from your 7-day prepay rule. Friday 4 December needs locking in by Friday 27 November, so the first email goes out this week.
  • Every function button opens an email to you, ready filled with “(from your email)” in the subject line and a short form for the date, numbers and space. You can see straight away which enquiries the emails sent you.
  • Smaller groups get the weekend Christmas roast and a link to book online. Gift cards get two emails: one in early December, and a last-minute one for anyone who leaves it late.
  • The emails come from you. They're written in your voice and signed by you, because people book functions with a person they trust.
  • Every price, date and space is from your Christmas menu, your function booklet and your October menu. The reviews quoted are from your Google listing (1,500+ five-star reviews).
  • The first send goes to your newsletter subscribers in the morning and everyone else that evening, so we can check it lands well before it goes to the whole list.

03Results

Added about a week after each send: deliveries, clicks and unsubscribes from Squarespace, and the function enquiries, bookings and gift cards that came through the emails.

$750 Month one to beat (from the first send to Christmas). More than $750 through the emails and month one's fee applies. Below it, month one is free.

How it's counted (from our agreement)

  • Functions: an enquiry that comes from one of these emails (subject line “from your email”, or a click on a function button) counts once it's confirmed with a deposit, within 30 days of the enquiry, at its confirmed value.
  • Table bookings: bookings made through a tracked link in the emails, with each guest valued at your average spend per head. It's the first question below.
  • Gift cards: gift cards bought through a tracked link in the emails.
  • The figures come from your own NowBookit, gift card and function records, and you'll see every booking and sale behind the total.
