Privacy
policy

What happens to your information when you use this site. Short, because there is not much of it.

What is collected

If you fill in the contact form, the name, email, business and message you type are sent to me by email. Nothing else on this site asks you for personal information.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. That data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

What is not done

Your details are never sold, rented or passed to anyone else. You are not added to any mailing list because you used the contact form. There is no advertising on this site and no advertising pixels.

How long it is kept

Enquiry emails are kept while there is a reason to keep them, and deleted when there is not. Ask me to delete yours at any time and it is gone.

Getting in touch

Any question about this, or any request to see or delete what is held about you, goes through the contact form.
