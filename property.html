The rules

What I never change.

Your name is on the listing. These photos are built so a buyer walking in on inspection sees the room they were shown.

The building

Walls, windows, doors, fireplaces, fixtures and finishes stay exactly where and what they are.

The condition

No hiding marks, cracks, damp or wear. If it's in the room, it's in the photo.

The outlook

No new views, no bluer skies over a different street, no removed power lines or neighbours.
