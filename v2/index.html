The Service Edit

You're not paying for pages. Five or fifty, you're paying for what they do.

A cheap site is a template with your logo on it. That's fine if you only need to exist online. If your website is meant to bring in work, the design is the last and smallest part of the job.

I build websites and email for trades, hospitality and local service businesses across Australia. Every build goes through the same five layers, and every layer has one job: turning attention into enquiries.

What every build has taught me

The five layers under a website that works.

A plasterer on the Gold Coast. A pizza bar in Casuarina. A mortgage broker in North Perth. A venue finder working worldwide. Different buyers, different problems, and the same five things decide whether the site brings in work. All of them get sorted before anyone sees a colour.

01 · Strategy

The expensive part starts before the design.

Who needs to find you, what they need to know, and what happens next. A builder checks crew capacity and documentation before sending plans. An EA booking a conference wants someone to trust with the decision. Someone looking for a broker wants to know exactly who they'll deal with. Get the buyer wrong and no design will save it.

02 · Copy

Good copy doesn't sound like everyone else.

"Quality you can trust" is on every trade site. "The specification comes first" tells a builder exactly what kind of subcontractor Coastside is. "The right venue isn't searched. It's sourced." says what CVBS does that a search box can't. Every generic line is cut until the site sounds like the business, not the internet.

03 · Search

The best website is useless if nobody finds it.

Google has to understand what you do and where you do it. For a trade that means services and service areas, structured and connected. For CVBS it meant a page for every destination from Sydney to the Hunter Valley, each linked to the services behind it. Search isn't something added afterwards. It is the architecture.

04 · Enquiries

Traffic isn't the end goal. Enquiries are.

Every site has one next step, and it's different every time. Send the plans. Submit a brief. Book a table. Book a chat. Every section either builds the case for that step or it comes out. Nothing sits on the page without a reason.

05 · Build

Designing it is only half the job.

A builder checks it on a phone on site. An EA checks it between meetings. Someone books a table from the couch. Pages that load fast, forms that send, layouts that hold at every screen size. The part nobody sees is the part that decides whether it works.
