Real builds

Live rebuilds for real businesses.

CVBS, Worldwide Venue Finding Solutions

The problem

An established, capable business with a site that didn't show it. The layout felt dated, and the core service wasn't clear in the first few seconds, so visitors couldn't tell at a glance what they did or why to trust them.

What we changed

A full rebuild with a clear structure, confident messaging that explains the service up front, and a clean, modern design that finally works properly on a phone.

Why it converts

When a site looks as credible as the service behind it, people stop second-guessing and start enquiring. Every section is built around that one decision.

Meat Merchant

The problem

A well-known Casuarina butcher with an online store that looked the part but didn't push people to buy. The path to actually ordering wasn't obvious, and there was little urgency or reassurance to turn a browser into a checkout.

What we changed

We rebuilt the store around how people actually buy meat: a clear "Build Your Box", easy subscriptions, honest sourcing front and centre, delivery zones made obvious, and real customer reviews where they count.

Why it converts

Every block now answers a buying question, from "is this good meat?" to "do you deliver to me?", so the store does the selling and the customer just has to add to box.

Fixing Carpentry WA

What's holding it back

Premium, high-end carpentry trusted by Perth builders and architects, but the business lives only on Facebook and Instagram. There's no real website to send a serious client to, and a social feed can't show the breadth or quality of the work the way it deserves.

The concept

A full website that leads with craftsmanship: clear services from finishing to decking and custom timber, real project galleries, and an effortless way for builders and homeowners to request a quote.

Why it works

High-end clients expect a high-end presence. A proper site turns "I found them on Instagram" into "this is clearly the company we trust with the finish."

Unsolicited concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Fixing Carpentry WA.

Tower Carpentry

What's holding it back

Beautiful bespoke joinery like media walls, fireplaces and custom doors, but the only home online is a social profile. There's nowhere to properly showcase the detail, and nothing that makes booking feel effortless.

The concept

A clean, photo-led site built around the signature work, with a clear quote request and the service areas across the Perth metro and Mandurah front and centre.

Why it works

Bespoke work sells on detail. A real site lets the craftsmanship carry the page and gives a serious enquiry somewhere to land.

Unsolicited concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Tower Carpentry.

King Carpentry WA

What's holding it back

Quality carpentry such as acoustic panelling, renovations and decking, across Perth's northern suburbs, but discoverable only through social. No website means missing the homeowners who search before they shortlist.

The concept

A considered site that names the services clearly, shows the work, and makes the northern-suburbs service area obvious, with a simple path to a quote.

Why it works

Most homeowners search before they call. Without a site, you're invisible at the exact moment people are deciding who to shortlist.

Unsolicited concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by King Carpentry WA.

JMA Group

What's holding it back

Two strong building brands under one roof, carpentry and construction plus new homes, with no single home online to tie them together. On social, the two offerings blur and the bigger story gets lost.

The concept

A clean group landing page that splits the two brands clearly and sends each visitor to the right place, ready to grow as the homes side launches.

Why it works

When a business has more than one offer, clarity wins work. A proper front door lets each brand stand on its own and look established.

Unsolicited concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by JMA Group.
