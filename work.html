A note on labels

Live means live

Sites marked Live are client businesses trading today. Concept means I built it to show what a business of that kind could look like, and no client has paid for it or signed off on it. In progress and Client build are exactly what they say.

Plenty of studios put concept work in a portfolio and let you assume. I would rather label it and have you trust the rest of the page.
